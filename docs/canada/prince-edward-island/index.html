How to create an export company account on ALWA

Follow these steps to set up a company account ready for publishing:

  1. Download the ALWA app from Google Play or the App Store
  2. Create an Office Account — the Office manages your companies
  3. Inside the Office, create a Company Account
  4. Fill in company data: name, country, Gmail, WhatsApp number
  5. Publish your export products with photos and available quantities
  6. Connect with buyers in Iraq, the Gulf, Europe and North America
